# Competitive Pressure Test Requirements

## DifferentiationHypothesis
Model the claimed material difference from an actual buyer alternative, why it matters, proof required, population, counterevidence, and experiment history. States: Proposed, Testing, Supported, Weak, Rejected, PopulationLimited. Proposed differentiation must never be emitted as an established marketing claim.

## Alternative-specific comparison
Comparison material must include when the alternative is appropriate. Required alternatives include established root-cause methods, internal senior experts, consulting, ADR/decision matrices, decision-intelligence systems, modernization assessments, technical-diligence specialists, generic AI/document chat, knowledge-management tools, and doing nothing.

## IndependencePolicy
Commercial motions may be IndependenceRequired, IndependencePreferred, or ImplementationCompatible. Conflicts and downstream implementation incentives must be explicit.

## BoundedDiagnostic
Define question, scope, exclusions, evidence/access required, duration hypothesis, deliverable, decision enabled, uncertainty targeted, stop conditions, price hypothesis, delivery effort, and follow-up window.

## UncertaintyReduction
Classify important uncertainties after work as Reduced, Resolved, Reframed, Increased, or RemainUnknown. Never manufacture confidence to demonstrate value.

## Actionability and socializability
Track authorized owner, feasible next action, authority/budget, dependencies, timing, internal-forwarding success, stakeholder disagreement, and requested simplification. Analytical success is separate from organizational actionability.

## EvidenceBurden
Track client effort required to supply evidence. Optimize total effort, not just Echelon effort.

## DecisionTiming
Qualification must identify the actual decision window.

## PoliticalValidationRisk
Represent sponsors seeking a predetermined conclusion or internal ammunition. Permit reframing, broader evidence, independence controls, or disqualification.

## EconomicValueOfInformation
Where practical, compare decision magnitude/reversibility with plausible value of reducing uncertainty. Avoid expensive diagnosis of cheap reversible decisions.

## ImplementationSeparation
Diagnosis may end successfully with no Echelon implementation. Implementation is a separate commercial decision.

## Comparative experiments
Clarity, EDF, and HelixNote must be tested against the buyer's actual alternative. Candidate outcomes: cycle time, traceability, contradictions found, rework avoided, comprehension, actionability, outcome quality, and total effort.

## Framework invisibility experiment
Every named framework must be tested against problem-forward messaging where its name is secondary or absent.

## Portable deliverable
Diagnostic outputs should be independently usable without ongoing subscription where appropriate. Portability is a trust hypothesis.

## Follow-through state
InterventionFollowThrough: NotPlanned, Planned, Started, Blocked, Completed, Abandoned, Unknown. Outcome learning distinguishes bad recommendation from no implementation.

## Commercial timing
Add DecisionWindow and TriggerUrgency independent of generic lead scoring.

## Proof gap projection
For each motion expose the highest-value missing proof: category comprehension, prevalence, willingness to talk, willingness to pay, repeatable delivery, measurable outcome, repeat purchase, referral, scalable acquisition, or economic scalability.
